# Canvas workspace redesign and Chrome verification

Date: 2026-09-14. Base: `d58a8f7`. Branch: `codex/kras-investigation`.

## Product review

The former layout gave the molecular view, findings, evidence and pair analysis similar visual weight. This obscured the primary task: inspect a structural context, select a residue or variant, connect it to measurements, and record a testable interpretation. Long forms and calculation details competed with the structure before the researcher needed them.

The redesign makes the molecular canvas the main workspace. A compact rail opens one contextual panel; the assay explorer expands beneath the structure. Measured outcomes come first in selection details. Comparison methods and sources have defined homes. Findings and experiments share a notebook with compact saved entries and explicit editing. A linked sequence, direct variant/residue search, region shortcuts, overlay/split views, and focus/reset controls connect these surfaces.

This is a UI and workflow change. The real KRAS case, existing scientific calculations, provenance, durable revision handling, recovery, imports and exports remain in use. Both experimental structures carry G12V in different binding contexts; selecting another substitution does not alter their coordinates or establish a causal mechanism.

- Centered native dialogs; made notebook controls fit the sidebar; clarified reference-validation wording and copy titles.
